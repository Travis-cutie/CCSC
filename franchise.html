06 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before applying. Can't find your answer? Our franchise team is happy to help.

01How do I become a Chicha San Chen partner?

Thank you for your interest in the Chicha San Chen brand. We hope to collaborate with partners around the world and bring our tea to the global market. To receive more information, please complete the Master Franchise Application online and our team will contact you.

02What's the difference between a master franchise and a sublicense?

Taking our company operations and market-development strategy into account, Chicha San Chen operates on a master franchise model.

  • Master franchise — granted per country, city or region. The master franchisee must have its own management team, open an agreed number of stores in its territory, and communicate directly with headquarters.
  • Sublicense — granted per store. Suited to partners without prior experience who want to start their own business; their main contact is the master franchisee for the area.
03How is a master franchise structured, and for how long?

Master franchises are discussed per country, city or region on a sole-franchise basis. The franchise period is five to eight years.

04What does the initial investment cover?

Initial capital includes the franchise fee, the store site, the build-out of the first store, equipment and raw materials for the first store, and the operating costs of the master franchise.

05What qualifications does a master franchisee need?
  • Alignment with the Chicha San Chen management philosophy.
  • Strong enthusiasm for the food and beverage industry — previous restaurant experience preferred.
  • Proven management skills.
  • An in-house management team covering marketing, operations, finance and more.
  • Knowledge of managing the region and familiarity with competing brands.
  • The ability to secure store sites.
06How long until someone contacts me after I apply?

Once we receive your complete Master Franchise Application, the preliminary assessment takes about 3–5 working days, after which our team will contact you.

07What is the franchise fee for my region?

Headquarters sets the franchise fee for each region based on its level of economic development and average per-capita spending.

Please complete the Master Franchise Application. After a preliminary qualification assessment and a visit to our concept store in Taichung, Taiwan, we'll begin the quotation process.

08Who can I talk to about a master franchise?

All master franchise matters are handled centrally by our headquarters in Taichung, Taiwan. Please complete the Master Franchise Application online and our team will reply as soon as possible. For other questions, contact our international affairs department at chichasanchen.global@teashop168.com.tw.

09How are raw materials supplied?

Chicha San Chen is committed to serving the best tea, guided by three principles:

  • Use only the highest-quality, natural ingredients.
  • Uphold food hygiene and safety.
  • Serve every customer fresh, freshly brewed products.

To keep products consistent across stores, all partners purchase key equipment and raw materials from headquarters. Provided quality and texture are unchanged, some milk, agricultural products and non-technical equipment may be sourced locally once approved by our R&D team.
